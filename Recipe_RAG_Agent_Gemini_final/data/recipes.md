# Recipe Knowledge Base

## 1. High-Protein Chicken Rice Bowl
Cuisine: Asian / Indian-inspired
Time: 30 minutes
Servings: 2

Ingredients:
- 300 g boneless chicken breast
- 1 cup cooked rice
- 1 tomato, chopped
- 1/2 onion, sliced
- 1/2 cup mixed vegetables
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chili powder
- Salt to taste
- Lemon juice

Method:
1. Cut chicken into small pieces and season with turmeric, chili powder and salt.
2. Heat oil in a pan and cook onion and ginger-garlic paste.
3. Add chicken and cook until fully cooked.
4. Add tomato and vegetables and cook for 5-7 minutes.
5. Serve over cooked rice and finish with lemon juice.

Substitutions:
- Chicken can be replaced with tofu for a vegetarian version.
- Brown rice or quinoa can replace white rice.
- Use gluten-free certified seasonings if required.

Nutrition estimate per serving:
Approximately 520 kcal, 42 g protein, 55 g carbohydrates and 14 g fat.

## 2. Vegetable Pasta
Cuisine: Italian
Time: 25 minutes
Servings: 2

Ingredients:
- 180 g pasta
- 1 cup tomato sauce
- 1 zucchini, sliced
- 1/2 bell pepper
- 1/2 onion
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp Italian herbs
- Salt and pepper

Method:
1. Boil pasta according to package directions.
2. Saute onion, zucchini and bell pepper in olive oil.
3. Add tomato sauce and herbs.
4. Add cooked pasta and mix well.
5. Serve warm.

Substitutions:
- Use gluten-free pasta for a gluten-free meal.
- Nutritional yeast can replace cheese for a dairy-free option.

Nutrition estimate per serving:
Approximately 430 kcal, 12 g protein, 68 g carbohydrates and 12 g fat.

## 3. Chickpea Salad
Cuisine: Mediterranean
Time: 15 minutes
Servings: 2

Ingredients:
- 1 can cooked chickpeas
- 1 cucumber
- 1 tomato
- 1/4 onion
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Lemon juice
- Salt, pepper and herbs

Method:
1. Rinse and drain chickpeas.
2. Chop vegetables.
3. Combine all ingredients.
4. Add lemon juice and herbs.
5. Toss and serve.

Substitutions:
- White beans can replace chickpeas.
- Use extra cucumber and tomato for a lighter version.

Nutrition estimate per serving:
Approximately 330 kcal, 12 g protein, 42 g carbohydrates and 13 g fat.
